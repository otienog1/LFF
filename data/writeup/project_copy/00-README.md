# Luigi Footprints Foundation — Projects Page Research Pack

Website-ready content for the LFF Projects page, built from a full review of the Foundation's Facebook page (facebook.com/luigifootprints) — every post from the first (1 July 2022) to the most recent (~mid-September 2026), roughly 250 posts.

The guiding rule throughout: **think in real-world projects, not Facebook posts.** A dozen posts about the same Samburu ranger housing became one project with a timeline — not a dozen entries. Nothing is invented; anything unconfirmed is parked in the "Needs Verification" file rather than written into the public copy.

## Two things to know up front
- **Images:** Facebook's signed CDN blocks reliable automated downloading (tested and confirmed). So `04-recommended-images.md` is a **sourcing catalogue** — it names the best images and the post to find each in, and suggests the fastest way to grab the originals (Meta Business Suite → Content, since you own the page). Tell me which images you want and I can capture clean screenshots and deliver them as files.
- **The big open question is now resolved:** Luigi is **Luigi Francescon (1948–2016)**, the Foundation's namesake, and the Amboseli elephant called Luigi was named after him. What remains open is smaller — mainly figures read from a photographed KWS speech, and the current Dignity Housing count. See `06-needs-verification.md`, which also now lists **conflicts between the live website and the Facebook record** (section E) — the site's "4 of 25" housing figure and Nairobi-only framing are the most out of date.
