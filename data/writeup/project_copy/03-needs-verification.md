# Read before publishing

Updated after the second pass. All nineteen projects now have copy; three new projects have been added. Below is what I'd still want confirmed, in rough order of how much it matters.

## 1. The mangrove project — written, but the Foundation is never named

You confirmed it: at the coast, run by SUSO, supported by LFF. The Maniago page has nothing on
mangroves, so the copy is sourced from **Stand Up Shout Out's own page**, where the work is
documented in detail — Jomvu Kuu and Tudor Creek in Mombasa, more than 10,000 seedlings planted from
2021, nurseries grown past 10,000, the Wasini Island expansion with Shimoni Turtle Watch and the
Wasini Women Group.

**But none of those posts mention the Luigi Footprints Foundation.** They credit SUSO, the community
groups, and in one case The Nature Conservancy in Africa. I have written the entry as the Foundation
supporting SUSO's Adopt A Creek programme, which is what you told me, but the public record on
Facebook does not show it. **Confirm you are comfortable claiming it in the Foundation's own voice**
— and if the support was of a particular kind or period (funding the nurseries, a specific planting),
tell me and I will say so precisely rather than generally.

I have also changed the slug from `planting-trees-in-samburu` to
**`planting-mangroves-at-the-coast`**, since you have settled that the title was the correct one.

**Poaching:** the copy now uses the later figure, as you directed — *"not one rhino had been poached
in Nairobi National Park in over seven years."* It appears in the Dignity Housing entry, where
Senior Warden Dadacha gives it to the visiting donors in September 2021, and again in the ranger

March 2021 launch post is not used anywhere.

**Cost:** the Dignity Housing entry now states twenty-five homes at **US$14,000 each — US$350,000**,
consistent with the per-home figure already on the site. The launch post's approximate US$250,000 is
no longer quoted.

One leftover: **the rhino count.** The March 2021 launch post says 120 rhinos in the park, the July
2020 World Rangers Day post says 124. Neither number now appears in any project copy — I removed the
124 rather than pick between them. If you want a figure on the site, tell me which, and whether it
should be the count today rather than either of those.
