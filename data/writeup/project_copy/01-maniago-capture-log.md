# Maniago Safaris page — capture log

Source: facebook.com/ManiagoSafarisEastAfrica, the page of the Foundation's primary supporter.
Captured 22–23 September 2026 by page-scoped search across all years.

Everything below is quoted or closely paraphrased from posts on that page. Dates are the post
dates Facebook reports. Where a post was published by a partner and shared, the author is named.

**One naming note that runs through all of it:** the Foundation is called the **"Luigi Francescon
Foundation"** in the 2020–2021 posts and the **"Luigi Footprints Foundation"** from late 2021
onward. Same body, renamed. Copy below uses the current name.

## Providing water to wildlife

- **August 2020 (World Elephant Day)** — "On World Elephant day, we focused our conservation efforts
  in Tsavo, where Kenya has the largest number of elephants. It is the dry season and there is a
  scarcity of water. So, through our Foundation, we hired **seven bowsers carrying 88,000 litres of
  water**, which were used to fill **three waterholes, a mud pit and 3 water troughs in Tsavo East**.
  This was carried out by the able bodied enthusiastic SUSO youth. **30 excited Susonians** were
  taken to Voi from Nairobi for this particular day, which also coincided with Youth Day. A thank
  you also goes to KWS."
- **September 2021** — "**8 bowsers with 96,000 litres of water** went into Tsavo to fill some water
  troughs and a dried up waterhole. The dry season is upon us."

## Sustaining community livelihoods during COVID-19

- **April 2020** — "Maniago Safaris and SUSO (Stand Up Shout Out) with funding by the Luigi
  Francescon Foundation have embarked on a **weekly food and book distribution to 500 families
  making up 3000 people** at the sprawling **Kibra slums** in Nairobi. Due to COVID 19 many people
  living in this area have lost their jobs… Apart from food items, we are also distributing books
  for the children to be able to continue to read and study while their schools are closed.
  **Spraying matatus (buses) and boda bodas** (motorbike taxis) is also vital… Large buckets of
  water with a tap… have been put in some areas for the use of people passing by."
- **April 2020 — "SUSTAINING KIBRA WEEK 3"** — "we sourced **fresh vegetables and fruit from nearby
  farms**, with the help of Anne Nganga… We have a **screening system** in place before anyone can
  enter the distribution centre at Kibra. Masks have to be worn and **special overalls** were made
  to keep the distributors safe."
- **May 2020 — fourth distribution** — "this distribution also focused on education material. We
  supplied **431 children with education packages, designed by their teachers**. This way they are
  able to study at home… there are no computers here so everything is manually done."
- **June 2020** — "George and James have been on a mission to deliver food, through our Luigi
  Francescon Foundation, to **needy communities near the Masai Mara**, who have been especially
  affected by the lack of tourism."
- **July 2020 — tenth distribution** — "Deliveries arrive for our **10th distribution** of essential
  goods and food to Kibra… **Thousands of people** have been the recipients of this aid."
- **September 2020** — "More action within Kibra. With the SUSO team, we have embarked on a
  **Community Challenge — a resilient community for a sustainable future**. The team cleaned up,
  painted and built pathways at the **Phoenix Library and the Three Bells Primary School**. A green

## Eradicating the invasive Parthenium weed

- **June 2020** — "The whole Maniago team along with SUSO and **Kibera slum residents, comprising of
  130 people**, sponsored by the Luigi Francescon Foundation, went into the Nairobi National park on
  Friday to start eradicating the deadly Parthenium weed that is invading the park grasslands…
  **We will be repeating the exercise twice a week for the next few months.** The Kiberians were very
  excited to be able to go into the park, which most of them had never done… **They earned a small
  wage**, which in these difficult times, where many have no jobs, was much appreciated… we were all
  very well protected by the Kenya Wild Life Services rangers."
- **June 2020** — "many Kenya guides, who are dedicated to conservation and the environment, came
  together to clean up the Nairobi National Park and help eradicate alien plants like the
  parthenium… Four of Maniago's guides… George, Peter, Mikhail and Stephen were also actively
  involved."
- **June 2022 (World Environment Day)** *(Stand Up Shout Out)* — "together with our key partners
  Maniago Safaris East Africa and Kenya Wildlife Service, we took time out to participate in a
  parthenium removal exercise at the **murram pits No. 8 to No. 9** at Nairobi National Park; where
  we managed to pull out **611kgs** of the invasive weed… **80 plus participants**."

## Dignity Housing — the real beginning

*Added in the second pass. This is the section that corrects the site's timeline.*

- **March 2021 — the launch post.** "We are very happy to introduce our latest conservation project,
  known as **Dignity Housing**. It all started when the Senior Warden of the Nairobi National Park,
  **Joseph Dadacha**, had a dream to upgrade the living accommodation from the current small tents
  with no beds or bathroom and kitchen facilities, for the 'rhino' rangers. These are the special
  rangers who live in the rhino blocks inside this park, which is a rhino sanctuary… thanks to these
  able bodied rangers, there has been **no poaching of rhinos in the Nairobi National Park in well
  over a year**. The success story of maintaining the **120 rhinos** residing in this park continues
  with the birth of several rhinos during the same period… **Maniago Safaris Ltd with our Luigi
  Footprints Foundation and Stand Up Shout Out (SUSO) came together** to support this initiative. We
  collectively designed and built **the first two self-contained container homes**, which have beds,
  a kitchenette and a full bathroom… Once there, we will install the water tanks and plumbing system
  as well as the solar power units which will give the rangers running water, warm showers and
  electricity within. **There are 25 rhino blocks within the park. We begin with these two container
  homes and will build the next 23 over a period of time. The cost of this entire project is
  approximately US$250,000.**"
- **April 2021** — "The Dignity Houses are going into place in the Nairobi National Park… The bio
  digester hole has been dug ready for it to go in. Next the plumbing and the solar lighting and
  water heating. **Bushes to be planted around them as camouflage.** The rhino rangers are looking
  forward to their new homes."
- **July 2021 (World Rangers Day)** — "Our Dignity Housing project is going ahead with the **3rd and
  4th houses** for the special rhino rangers going into place in the Nairobi National Park **during
  August**."
- **September 2021** — "Visiting and inspecting the Luigi Footprints Foundation Dignity Housing units
  for the special rhino rangers inside the Nairobi National Park, with **Martin Meyer and potential
  donors**… Senior Warden Joseph Dadacha briefed them on the role of these particular rangers, who
  have, through their great determination, special training and pure passion, succeeded in keeping
  the rhinos very safe in the Park, **as there has not been any poaching there in over 7 years**."

# Coastal mangroves — sourced from Stand Up Shout Out's page

*The Maniago page has nothing on mangroves. You confirmed the project was at the coast, run by SUSO
with the Foundation's support, so these come from facebook.com/standupshoutoutworld. The Foundation
is not named in any of them — see item 1 of the verification file.*

- **May 2022** — "our SUSO Mombasa team did a site visit to **Jomvu Kuu conservation area** where SUSO
  has managed to plant **over 10,000 Mangrove seedlings since 2021**. We planted various species of
  mangroves such as the **Rhizophora mucronata and Avicennia marina** which are most popular around
  this areas… We have also planted in **Tudor Creek**… We appreciate this healthy partnership with
  **Bidii Creek Community conservation**." *(Hashtags: #AdoptAPark #AdoptACreek #SUSOMombasa.)*
- **July 2023** — "Mangroves… are disappearing 3 times faster than forests. We have **collected and
  nursed 5000 mangroves at Jomvu Kuu, Tudor Creek**."
- **August 2023** — "Our **Adopt A Creek** program is sailing smoothly as this past week we increased
  one of our mangrove nurseries to **over 10,000 seedlings worth over half a million KSH**… we have
  Adopt A Creek projects **from Kwale to Kilifi** coming up and ongoing."
- **September 2023** — "Our Stand Up Shout Out **Adopt A Creek and Mangrove Restoration Program** was
  expanded to **Wasini Island, South Coast** of Kenya in partnership with **Shimoni Turtle Watch and
  Wasini Women Group**. The mangrove nursery was set up in Wasini village… The species… are
  **Ceriops tagal**. We managed to set up **1050 seedlings** in a nursery."
- **Mangrove Day post (undated in capture)** — SUSO's own explainer on why they matter: mangroves
  reduce wave energy by up to 66%, store up to four times more carbon than most tropical forests on
  land, and up to 75% of commercially caught fish species spend part of their life cycle in mangrove
  roots; more than a third of the world's mangrove forests have been lost.
