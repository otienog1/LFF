# Luigi Footprints Foundation — Recommended Images (by project)

## About this catalogue — please read

Facebook serves its images from a signed content-delivery network (fbcdn) that blocks automated downloading from outside the app. I tested this during the review and confirmed the images can't be reliably pulled programmatically in this session — so rather than hand you a folder of half-failed or broken downloads, this document is a **sourcing catalogue**: for each project it names the strongest, most representative images and tells you exactly which post to find them in, so you (or a developer) can save the originals directly.

**How to save the originals (fastest reliable method):**
1. Open the LFF page: facebook.com/luigifootprints
2. Find the post named below (dates are given for each).
3. Open the image full-size and save it (right-click → Save image, or the download option on the post's photo view).
Because you're an admin/owner of the page, you can also bulk-download originals from **Meta Business Suite → Content**, or from the page's **Photos** albums, which keeps full resolution.

If you'd like, I can take clean screenshots of any specific images through the browser and deliver those as files — just tell me which projects or posts to grab, and I'll capture them.

**Selection principles used below:** the strongest, clearest image of the project, its people, the work completed, or its impact — avoiding near-duplicates. Each project lists a suggested **hero image** plus a few supporting shots.
