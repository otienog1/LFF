# Luigi Footprints Foundation — Consolidated Project List & Internal Inventory

*Built from a full review of the LFF Facebook page (facebook.com/luigifootprints), earliest post 1 July 2022 through mid-September 2026 (~250 posts). Every real-world project below groups all related posts — visits, updates, follow-ups and milestones — into a single entry, so no project is duplicated.*

**About the Foundation (from the page):** Luigi Footprints Foundation (LFF) is a Kenyan NGO working in wildlife conservation, community development and education. Its recurring themes are *"Thriving People, Thriving Wildlife,"* *"Room to Roam,"* and *"Dignity for Communities, Protection for Wildlife."* It works most closely with **Maniago Safaris**, **Stand Up Shout Out (SUSO)**, **ifaw**, the **Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS)** and **Samburu National Reserve**. Its matriarch/patron is **Annabella Francescon** (a KWS Honorary Warden). Website: theluigifootprints.org.

## The 12 consolidated projects (grouped by scale)

### Flagship & major programmes

**1. Dignity Housing Project** — *Ranger housing*
Building dignified, permanent homes for wildlife rangers, replacing tents and uniports. Sites documented: **Amboseli**, **Nairobi National Park (Rhino Rangers)**, and **Samburu National Reserve**. A KWS speech in the page states the programme plans **25 units** in total. *(Consolidates ~40+ posts across all three sites and the full construction-to-handover story.)*

**2. Olchani Project** — *Community tree nurseries & landscape restoration (Amboseli)*
Community-led restoration under the banner **#TreesForPeople**. "Olchani" means *"tree"* in the Maa language. Women-run nurseries (Osoit, Ildepen, Tembo and others), the **Orregie Enclosure** (a planned 5,000–7,000 indigenous-tree forest inside Amboseli NP), and tree-growing outreach into households and schools. Lead funder/partner: **ifaw**. *(Consolidates ~30+ posts.)*

**3. Ubuntu Hay** — *Community hay bank / drought resilience (Lemongo, Amboseli)*
A community-owned hay bank ("Olchani Phase 2") that stores fodder in good seasons to protect livestock and grasslands in drought. Launched mid-2025; first harvest/baling operation ~Sept 2025. Partner: ifaw. *(Consolidates ~7 posts.)*

**4. Ubuntu Smiles** — *Women's economic empowerment*
Economic-dignity programme empowering community women (beadwork/crafts and nursery income) toward financial independence. Partner: ifaw. *(Consolidates several women-empowerment posts.)*

**5. Outdoor Classroom Series** — *Conservation education for children*
Hands-on conservation lessons for primary-school children, led by community coexistence leaders at the community tree nurseries (e.g. Osoit with Nickson Seleon; Ildepen with Anna Tova). Part of the Olchani programme. *(Consolidates ~3–4 posts.)*

**6. Education & Scholarships** — *"Luigi's legacy"*
Supporting students through scholarships, conservation education and nurturing of talents. Documented: three girls — **Hope, Fantasia (Fantesia) and Sarah** — sponsored from Oldoinyo Primary School, Amboseli. *(Consolidates ~8 posts.)*

**7. Ranger Support & Recognition** — *Beyond housing*
The wider ranger-welfare commitment that complements Dignity Housing: World Ranger Day celebrations (2024, 2025, 2026), donated **camera/documentation equipment** for Samburu, support for **EarthRanger** wildlife-monitoring technology, and tree-planting alongside rangers. *(Consolidates ~10 posts; overlaps with Dignity Housing.)*

**8. Youth in Conservation & the SUSO Partnership** — *Community/youth environmental action*
Youth "changemakers" restoring landscapes: the **Adopt-A-Park** programme (with SUSO/KWS), **Mazingira Day** invasive-species (Parthenium) removal at Nairobi NP, the **Loitokitok town clean-up**, and youth tree-growing/mentorship. *(Consolidates ~10 posts.)*

**9. Nanare Art Challenge & Art for Change** — *Youth leadership through art*
A 2025 immersive leadership-development experience through art for 30 teenagers from Kakamega at the SUSO Mabingwa Youth Centre, plus earlier art experiences with youth (e.g. Kibera) hosted with visiting artists. *(Consolidates ~5 posts.)*

**10. Living Safely With Wildlife (Human–Wildlife Coexistence Training)** — *Coexistence*
Co-sponsored/hosted coexistence "toolbox" trainings (with **Save the Elephants**), equipping community ambassadors — including women leaders from SUSO Coexistence Villages — to reduce human–wildlife conflict. *(Consolidates ~3 posts, 2025 + 2026.)*

### Smaller initiatives & emergency response

**11. Drought Relief & Emergency Response** — *Nairobi NP & Amboseli (2022–2023)*
Early emergency work: delivering hay, lucerne and water (bowsers) to sustain wildlife and livestock during drought, at Nairobi National Park and across the Amboseli ecosystem. Foundational to the Foundation's story. *(Consolidates ~6 posts, plus a March-2023 reference in a KWS speech.)*

**12. Sea Turtle Nest Adoption** — *Diani coast*
On World Ocean Day (June 2025), LFF adopted a sea-turtle nest at Diani via Diani Turtle Watch / Local Ocean. *(Consolidates 2 posts.)*

### 1. Dignity Housing Project
- **Category:** Wildlife conservation / ranger welfare (infrastructure)
- **Location:** Amboseli; Nairobi National Park; Samburu National Reserve (Archers Post Gate area) · **Country:** Kenya
- **Beneficiaries:** KWS and Reserve wildlife rangers (incl. Rhino Rangers, Nairobi NP)
- **What LFF supported:** Fully contained ~20-ft housing units (kitchenettes, mounted beds, shelves, washrooms), fitted with water tanks and solar. Furnishing, tree-planting around homes, official handovers.
- **Key activities:** Fundraising; construction; handovers; ranger recognition; tree planting at sites.
- **Dates/milestones:**
  - 2022–2024: earliest Dignity Houses (Amboseli; Nairobi NP rhino rangers). Rhino-ranger house appeals from Nov 2024 & earlier.
  - 3 Jul 2025 — **House No. 5** launched, Nairobi NP (Phase 3), speech by KWS's Michael Nicholson on behalf of DG Prof. Erustus Kanga.
  - 17 Oct 2025 — **6th Dignity House** handed to Rhino Rangers, Nairobi NP.
  - Samburu: 4 Oct 2025 partnership meeting ("Bridges of Conservation") → 8–9 Oct announcements → **6 Nov 2025 groundbreaking** → 6 Dec construction begins → 27 Dec progress visit → 13 Jan 2026 building + tree planting → ~late Jan/early Feb 2026 official opening (Governor **Jonathan Lati Lelelit** opens it) → 9 Feb 2026 "Strengthening Security" (8 units, kitchen, washrooms, bridge crossing) → 11–14 Feb 2026 (connection, Valentine's) → 30–31 Jul 2026 World Ranger Day tree planting at the Samburu Dignity Housing.
- **Partners/people:** Annabella Francescon (donor of Samburu units); Maniago Safaris; SUSO; KWS (DG Prof. Erustus Kanga; Michael Nicholson); Tom Lesarge (Director, Samburu NR); Benson Omolo (contractor); Peter Karanja; Daniel (Daniel Gatumbo).
- **Images:** Many (ranger houses w/ solar, interiors — bunk beds, kitchens, rhino mural; handovers; before/after tents). See Recommended Images.
- **Missing/uncertain:** Exact count/locations of Houses 1–4; the "25 planned units" figure (from a photographed KWS speech — verify).

### 2. Olchani Project
- **Category:** Conservation / community development (restoration) · **Location:** Amboseli ecosystem (Meshenani, Lemongo, Orregie; nurseries at Osoit, Ildepen, Tembo) · **Country:** Kenya
