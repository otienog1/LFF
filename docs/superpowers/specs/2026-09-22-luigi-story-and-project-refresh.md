# Luigi's story and the project refresh: proposal for review

Date: 22 September 2026. Prepared for the site owner and the Luigi Footprints Foundation before any of it goes live.

Save as `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-22-luigi-story-and-project-refresh.md`.

The source for everything below is a full review of the foundation's Facebook page — every post from the first, on 1 July 2022, to September 2026, about 250 in all — consolidated into real-world projects rather than posts. It is committed at `data/writeup/project_copy/`; `06-needs-verification.md` carries the open questions and, in its section E, the conflicts between this site and that record.

## What the audit found

- **The site's project data has drifted four years behind the work.** The ten archived projects are inherited WordPress content from 2021 and 2022. The field record runs to September 2026 and contains at least six projects the site has never mentioned.
- **The flagship is the worst of it.** Dignity Housing is told as a Nairobi National Park project with four homes built. Since that copy was written a sixth house was handed to the Rhino Rangers at Nairobi (17 October 2025), and an eight-unit cluster was built at Samburu National Reserve — ground broken in November 2025, opened in early 2026 by the Governor of Samburu County. The Impact page already names Amboseli, Nairobi and Samburu for trees planted; only housing is still described as if it never left Nairobi.
- **The figure is stale in three coupled places.** `4 of 25` appears as an Impact figure in `impact-stats`, as `progress: { done: 4, total: 25, unit: "homes built" }` on the `dignity-housing` donate cause, and in prose in the Our Work `coexistence` block — *"Four of the park's 25 ranger camps have been housed so far."* Pass 7 deliberately read the projects flagship figure from the Impact page so it is written once; that property must survive this change.
- **Luigi Francescon is on the site; the elephant named after him is not.** `/about` tells his story well — the hero, the `legacy` block, the `about-luigi-panel` with his dates. What it never says is that a bull elephant in Amboseli carries his name, that his mother Lemlepo heads a family of around forty, and that the foundation has followed his life since 2022. That is the thread between the man on the About page and the rangers, nurseries and hay bank everywhere else on the site, and it is missing.
- **The Olchani programme is described more thinly than it is run.** "Four community-run nurseries" is accurate but anonymous; the nurseries have names — Osoit, Ildepen, Tembo — and the Orregie Enclosure inside Amboseli National Park is a named restoration site intended as a small forest of several thousand indigenous trees. ifaw is the lead funder and partner throughout and is nowhere credited.

## Direction

- **The elephant who carries the name.** A `content` block (`about-namesake`) on `/about` immediately after `about-luigi-panel`, rendered by a new `components/about/Namesake.tsx`: the mirror of the legacy chapter, text on the left six columns and the photograph on the right five, so the man's chapter and the elephant's face each other across the quote panel between them. The quote panel was not reused for it: its component hardcodes chapter 03 and the label "In his words", and its display type is sized for a two-sentence quote, not sixty words. The chapter takes number 04; principles and trustees move to 05 and 06. The copy is the longer version from `data/writeup/project_copy/02-projects-page-content.md`, adapted to the site's voice, and it gives the elephant's age as nine in May 2025 and ten now (see below). The photograph is the home hero's elephant herd, on the owner's instruction, as a placeholder until the foundation supplies one of Luigi himself; its alt text claims nothing about which elephants they are.
- **Dignity Housing across three landscapes.** The project write-up and excerpt widen from Nairobi to Amboseli, Nairobi and Samburu. Samburu becomes the closing chapter and the strongest one: a build documented from a partnership meeting by the Ewaso Nyiro River, through groundbreaking with Reserve Director Tom Lesarge and contractor Benson Omolo, to an opening by the Governor; eight units at a bridge crossing that had never had a permanent ranger base, so the crossing can now be staffed. The flagship stays the flagship and `FLAGSHIP_SLUG` does not move.
- **The housing figure.** Moved from `4 of 25` to `6 of 25` on the owner's instruction, on the strength of the foundation's own post of 17 October 2025 ("the 6th Dignity House was handed over to the Rhino Rangers of Nairobi National Park"). The figure stays a Nairobi figure: its description now reads "Ranger camps in Nairobi National Park housed so far through Dignity Housing, with an eight-unit base added at Samburu", the donate progress unit becomes "camps housed at Nairobi National Park" so the bar cannot be read as a programme-wide total, and the Our Work prose says six of Nairobi's 25 camps with the Samburu base named separately. The Amboseli count and whether twenty-five still frames the programme remain for the foundation to confirm (below). The write-up itself carries no running count, only the dated milestones, so the figure is still written once.
- **New projects into the archive.** Ubuntu Hay (the community hay bank at Lemongo, Olchani's second phase), Ubuntu Smiles (women's economic empowerment), the Outdoor Classroom Series (conservation lessons for schoolchildren at the nurseries), the Nanare Art Challenge (leadership through art for thirty teenagers from Kakamega), Living Safely With Wildlife (coexistence training, with Save the Elephants) and the Mazingira Day work at Nairobi National Park. Samburu is a chapter of Dignity Housing, not a project of its own. Entries follow the shape of the existing ones. **Convention for the WordPress-shaped fields:** a field entry's `id` is its slug, `databaseId` is omitted (the `Project` type now marks it optional) and `typesOfProjects` is an empty list, which one legacy entry already uses. No post ids are minted for content that never had a WordPress post; the legacy entries keep their export untouched, and nothing on the site reads those fields. The content tests assert the split: legacy entries carry a numeric `databaseId`, field entries use the slug as `id`, and ids are unique across the archive. Each field entry has an excerpt and a short write-up, so each gets its own page; each links from the programme it belongs to (restoration lists Ubuntu Hay and the Outdoor Classroom, enterprise lists Ubuntu Smiles, education the Nanare Art Challenge, coexistence the Living Safely With Wildlife training, and the Impact page's crisis chapter Mazingira Day). Dates: Ubuntu Hay 5 August 2025, Living Safely With Wildlife 18 May 2025, Mazingira Day 11 October 2025 and the Outdoor Classroom 17 February 2026 are documented days; Ubuntu Smiles (November 2025) and the Nanare Art Challenge (September 2026) are month-precision in the record and carry the first of the month.
- **Photographs for the field entries.** One wordless placeholder, `public/projects/placeholder.svg` (a paper-toned frame with a small camera mark, so it reads the same in every language), is the featured image of all six. Each entry's alt text starts "Photograph to come:" and names the post to source the photograph from, in all three languages; the tests assert both. The write-up's own photographs were not affected. The locale script now also translates the `alt` attributes inside write-up HTML, since the retold Dignity Housing write-up carries real alt text where the WordPress export had none.
- **Olchani named properly.** The nurseries and the Orregie Enclosure by name in the programme text and the home card, with the 5,000 to 7,000 tree figure from the foundation's own post. ifaw credited by name on the owner's instruction, as funder and partner of Olchani, Ubuntu Hay, Ubuntu Smiles and the Outdoor Classroom, styled lowercase as the organisation styles itself.
- **The legacy projects stay.** Dignity Tailors, the Inkaramatak Ildepen Maasai Ushanga shop, the Amboseli OYC Choir, the COVID-19 livelihoods work, the boots handover and the Lake Nakuru clean-up all predate the Facebook record. Their absence from the research is not evidence that they ended, and nothing here removes them.

## What is verified and what is not

The donate pass set the rule for this repo — no invented costs — and the same rule governs content. Stating it plainly, as that pass did:

**Verified, and safe to publish.** Luigi Francescon, 1948–2016, and the foundation founded in his memory (the site's own About page). Luigi the elephant as the foundation's namesake elephant in Amboseli, his mother Lemlepo, the LD family of around forty, no poaching incidents reported across the ecosystem for several years (the foundation's own posts). The sixth Dignity House handed to the Rhino Rangers at Nairobi, October 2025. Eight units, a kitchen and washrooms at Samburu, at a bridge crossing without a permanent base, opened by H.E. Jonathan Lati Lelelit, Governor of Samburu County. The nursery names, the Orregie Enclosure, and the named people below.

**Verified on the owner's instruction, 22 September 2026.** Six Dignity Houses at Nairobi National Park, from the foundation's post of 17 October 2025, now the published figure. ifaw as Olchani's funder and partner, now credited. The elephant's age: the owner confirms he turned nine in May 2025 and is ten in 2026, as the copy file (`02`) and the project list (`01`) say; the capture log's dating of the "turns 9 this May" post (`07`, post P12) to 2026 is the error. The site says he turned nine in May 2025 and is ten now.

**Not verified, and not to be published until the foundation confirms it.** The programme-wide total of Dignity Houses, the Amboseli count, and whether the twenty-five denominator still applies. The "25 planned units" figure and the five-park Adopt-A-Park list, both read from a photograph of a printed KWS speech rather than from the foundation. Luigi the elephant's birth year: nine in May 2025 implies 2016, the year Luigi Francescon died; the posts never draw that connection and the site does not either. The spelling Anna Touwa is used throughout (the record has both Touwa and Tova). The impact figures (2,553+ students, 3,500+ trees, 42,000 seedling capacity) are all likely low now, but a stale figure is better than an invented one.

**Images.** Facebook serves its images from a signed CDN that blocks automated downloading, so none of the new projects arrive with photographs. `04-recommended-images.md` names the strongest image for each project and the post it comes from; the foundation can export the originals from Meta Business Suite. Until then new entries carry the marked placeholder described above. No existing photograph is reused for a project it does not belong to — the Get Involved pass established that the photographs are honest to what they illustrate, and that holds here. The one exception is deliberate and the owner's: the home hero's elephant herd stands beside the elephant chapter on About until a photograph of Luigi is supplied, with alt text that claims nothing about which elephants they are.

## What the foundation needs to supply

1. **The Dignity Housing count.** The site now says six of twenty-five Nairobi camps, from the 17 October 2025 post. Confirm the Nairobi count, the Amboseli count, and whether the programme is still counted against twenty-five Nairobi ranger camps or against a new total now that Samburu is built.
2. **ifaw is now credited by name** as Olchani's funder and partner, on the owner's instruction. Confirm ifaw is content to be named on the public site, and the styling (lowercase "ifaw" is used).
3. **Current impact figures** — students reached, trees planted, nursery count and seedling capacity — or confirmation that the present ones still stand.
4. **Luigi the elephant's year of birth.** The owner confirms he turned nine in May 2025 and is ten now, and the site says so. Whether to draw the connection to 2016, the year Luigi Francescon died, is the foundation's call.
5. **Spelling of two names that appear both ways in the record:** Anna Tova / Anna Touwa (the site uses Touwa throughout), and Peter Fredrick Moll's relationship to the foundation as distinct from Stand Up Shout Out.
6. **A photograph for each new project, and one of Luigi the elephant** for the About chapter, which currently shows the home hero's elephant herd as a placeholder; or permission to leave the placeholders until the Meta export is done.
7. **Whether the Samburu opening date** is late January or early February 2026; the posts give both a Governor's appreciation on 31 January and an opening-day post without a visible date. The site says "early 2026".

## Validation

Run on 22 September 2026, on the working tree that became the `luigi-story-and-project-refresh` branch.

- **Typecheck.** `yarn tsc --noEmit` clean.
- **Tests.** `yarn test`: 3 files, 30 tests, all green. The content tests were changed deliberately: the archive count goes from 10 to 16; "only Dignity Housing has a write-up" becomes "Dignity Housing and the six field entries have write-ups"; the Dignity Housing parse now asserts six paragraphs (the tents, the programme, Nairobi, the Samburu build, the Samburu opening, the cost) and the same five images, with "Nairobi National Park" in the first paragraph, the bold "Dignity Housing" kept and "Samburu" present, in place of the old "25 camps" assertion. Four new tests: field entries use the slug as id and have no `databaseId` while legacy entries keep a numeric one and ids are unique; field entries carry the placeholder and "Photograph to come" alt text; the Impact figure reads `6 of 25` / `6 de 25` in all three locales and the donate cause's progress agrees with it.
- **Translations.** `python scripts/locale-copy.py` reports `es: 0 strings still in English` and `pt: 0 strings still in English` after 51 entries per locale were added and 24 stale lines dropped. The two donate causes that repeat the home card summaries (restoration, coexistence) were aligned to the new card copy so nothing English remained.
- **Browser, on the running dev server, headless Chrome.** `/about` at 1440: chapters read 02 The legacy, 03 In his words, 04 The elephant who carries the name, 05 What guides us, 06 Board of Trustees; no console errors or warnings; every request 200. `/es/about` and `/pt/about`: the fourth chapter localised, no English tokens in it. `/projects`: flagship figure `6 of 25` with the new description, the write-up's first two paragraphs, and the archive led by the six field entries on the placeholder, the legacy entries following. `/projects/ubuntu-hay`: placeholder hero, three write-up paragraphs, the three "more projects" all field entries. `/pt/projects/dignity-housing-for-wildife-rangers`: six translated paragraphs, five translated alt texts, `6 de 25` in the sponsor chapter. `/es/donate/dignity-housing`: progress bar labelled "6 de 25 campamentos alojados en el Parque Nacional de Nairobi". `/our-work`: the restoration text names ifaw, the three nurseries and the Orregie Enclosure; restoration lists Ubuntu Hay and the Outdoor Classroom, coexistence lists Living Safely With Wildlife. No horizontal overflow on any of these. `/pt/about` at the narrowest window the harness allows (490px): the chapter stacks text above the photograph, no overflow.
- **Not run.** A reduced-motion pass was not performed in this session; the new chapter uses only the existing `Photo`, `SplitHeading` and `ChapterMark` primitives, whose reduced-motion behaviour was verified in the earlier passes, and adds no motion of its own.
- **Production export.** `yarn build` (output: export) succeeded in a copy of the tree, because a build in the checkout would have shared `.next` with the running dev server. Yarn PnP resolves its global cache relative to the tree's depth on disk, so the copy had to sit beside the repo (`C:\Users\otien\build\LFF-buildcopy`) with the `.yarn` folder copied, not linked; it was deleted afterwards. The export contains `about.html`, `es/about.html` and `pt/about.html`, all six field project pages in all three locales, `projects/placeholder.svg`, and the figure in each locale's projects page.
